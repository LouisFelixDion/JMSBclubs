Wondering which club to join?

There is no single recruitment date for every club.

Each club sets its own openings, roles and application process. The safest approach is to choose the clubs that interest you, follow their official accounts and begin checking before each academic term starts.

Browse all clubs Official Concordia directory ↗

When should you look?

Fall intake
Start checking in late summer and continue through the first weeks of fall classes.
Winter openings
Check again before the winter term and during its first weeks.
Early recruitment
Some programs begin much earlier. JMCC states that general recruitment for selected disciplines runs from April through September.

How the process usually works

1

Choose a club

Compare its focus, events and available roles.

2

Watch for openings

Follow the club’s official account and recruitment announcements.

3

Apply

Complete the club’s form and provide any requested documents.

4

Interview or try out

Some roles use interviews, cases or tryouts. Others do not.

5

Join the team

Selected applicants receive the club’s onboarding details.

Before applying

  • Read the club’s mandate and recent event posts.
  • Know which role or function interests you.
  • Prepare a short explanation of why you want to join.
  • Update your résumé if the application requests one.
  • Use specific examples for interview questions.

Leadership and contacts

President names change each academic year. Current names will appear here only after they are verified.

Until then, use each club’s profile and official Instagram account for recruitment questions and current deadlines.
