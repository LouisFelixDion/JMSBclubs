When is recruitment?

September to October
First-Year Reps at almost every club, plus most member programs (analysts, delegates, ambassadors).
February to May
Executive teams for next year. CASA clubs usually open applications in February to March.
Year-round
Volunteers and some programs open whenever a club needs people. Check each club's Instagram.
